Comparing the Data

By comparing race data from both crashes, we can look for patterns in what went wrong.

Speed

Looking at how fast Leclerc was going into the corner in each crash shows whether he was carrying too much speed and where he started to lose control.

Throttle

Throttle data shows how hard he was pushing on the gas through the corner. Getting on the throttle too early at the Parabolica can cause the car to slide.

Car Position

Tracking the car's position on the track shows the exact line he took each time and where the car left the track.

What the Crashes Have in Common

Putting the data together helps explain why the Parabolica is so tricky. Even for a top driver like Leclerc, the mix of high speed and a long corner leaves very little room for error, which is likely why he crashed there twice.
